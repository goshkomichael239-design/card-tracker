# Sounds: sources and licences

All files are mono mp3, normalised to a -3 dB peak; the app plays them much
quieter. Every source is CC0 (public domain) or was made for this project, so no
credit is required. The names are `<event>-<n>.mp3`; the app picks one of the
numbers at random and never repeats the last one.
